Privacy Policy — Leo G Audit

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Leo G Audit is an internal tool used by Sunwest / SW Construction operations assistants to read QuickBooks Online accounting data for reconciliation and audit, with authorization from the company administrator (Jesús).

Data we access

When connected, the app may read QuickBooks Online company data such as company info, customers, invoices, payments, and standard financial reports. It does not request payroll or payment-processing scopes beyond accounting read needs as configured by the administrator.

How data is used

Data is used only to audit and reconcile books against the company’s internal ERP, at the direction of authorized staff. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely for the administrator’s assistant environment to maintain the connection. Tokens can be revoked by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks or Intuit Connected Apps.

Contact

Questions: contact the company administrator who authorized this app.
